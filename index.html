What we do

Club Day, every Thursday

We meet in the club room in the main building. We go through technique, composition and lighting, then take on a photography challenge together.

Photo walks

Bring any camera. If you are new, you walk with someone who has been shooting longer.

Critique nights

Members bring photos to talk about. New members pick up their member card at the next one.

Workshops and the exhibition

We teach composition and light before gear, and members lend each other lenses and tripods. Each year we hold an exhibition.

Questions people ask first

Do I need a real camera?

No. A phone is fine for most walks and critiques. Lenses and tripods get passed around between members, and we start with composition and light before talking about gear.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Membership is free, and there are no annual or hidden fees.

I have never taken a serious photo. Is that a problem?

No. Most members started the same way, and on walks you are paired with someone more experienced.

When do you meet?

On Thursdays, in the club room in the main building. We call it Club Day.

Who owns the photos you show?

The photographer does. We ask before using anyone's photo on this site, in print or on social media.
