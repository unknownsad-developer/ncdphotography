Open the form, add up to three photos and tell us a little about them. We keep your original file at full quality.

Who can enter
NCD students and club members
Limit
Three photos each month
Size
At least 2,000 pixels on the long side
Closes
The 24th of each month
Open the form

How a photo is chosen

  1. You send your photos through the form.
  2. Two committee members check each entry against the rules.
  3. Three judges score the shortlist without seeing any names.
  4. The highest score becomes the Picture of the month on the 1st.
